AI video dubbing for YouTube

How DUBSEE turns supported YouTube speech into an AI voice, what DubSee Pro adds, and what the dubbing does not do.

Bu sayfayı Türkçe oku

AI video dubbing, in this app, means one job: take the spoken content of a supported YouTube video, translate it, and play that translation with a synthetic voice while you watch. It is not a studio recording, a human casting session, or a subtitle file you export. The thing you hear is speech, generated for the language you picked.

What you hear

You choose the target language. DUBSEE plays the dubbed voice with the video. The App Store listing also says you can switch back to the original audio, which matters when a term is clearer in the source language than in the translation. On first launch the app may download an advanced voice pack; until that download finishes, dubbing can be missing even though the link is valid.

Ten advanced voices are part of DubSee Pro, not a claim that every free session offers a voice menu of ten. Background and lock-screen listening are also Pro features. Free playback stays inside the daily video count and the roughly 18-minute limit.

Where dubbing helps

A lecture, a conference talk, a cooking or woodworking tutorial, and a podcast episode uploaded to YouTube are the cases where a spoken translation is more useful than pausing to read. The picture stays in front of you. The voice carries the explanation. DUBSEE does not become a newsroom, a film catalog, or a remote control because the video happens to be about those subjects.

Limits that stay true

No Shorts. No live streams. No promise for videos without captions. No offline library of arbitrary YouTube downloads. No unlimited free use. DubSee Pro is the plan that removes the duration cap and the ads, unlocks unlimited videos, adds the 10 voices, and turns on background playback. If the App Store does not show a trial, there is no trial to describe here.

Questions

Is the dubbed voice a recording of a person?

No. DUBSEE plays an AI voice for the translation. It is not a cast dubbing session and it does not hand you a subtitle file.

Can I listen with the screen locked?

Background and lock-screen playback are part of DubSee Pro. Free playback does not include that, and free videos are about 18 minutes.
