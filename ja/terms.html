1. What DUBSEE is

DUBSEE is a personal utility that helps you hear online videos with on-device dubbing in many languages. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any third-party video platform.

3. Content

Video content belongs to its respective owners and platforms. DUBSEE does not claim ownership of that content. Dubbing audio prepared for you is for personal listening on your device.

4. Features

DUBSEE has a free plan with 1 video a day, plus up to 2 more after watching rewarded ads, and an about 18-minute limit on free videos. DUBSEE Pro unlocks unlimited videos, removes the duration limit and ads, adds 10 voices, and enables background and lock-screen playback. Pro is offered as monthly and yearly App Store subscriptions. A free trial appears only when the App Store confirms eligibility. Features may change over time.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. After a free trial, the plan renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel the subscription in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Deleting your DUBSEE account does not cancel the Apple subscription.
