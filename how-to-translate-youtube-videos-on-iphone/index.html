How to translate YouTube videos on iPhone

Copy a supported YouTube link, paste it into DUBSEE, choose a language, and listen on your iPhone. What to try if the dubbing does not start.

Bu sayfayı Türkçe oku

This is the path that actually exists in DUBSEE. There is no account step on the website, and there is no box on this page that dubs a video for you. The translation happens in the iPhone app after you paste a link.

Before you paste

Use a regular YouTube video, not a Short and not a live stream. Captions need to be available for that video. A link that only opens a channel page, a playlist with nothing selected, or a clip is not the input the app is built for. Copy the video link from YouTube’s share sheet.

The three steps

  1. Copy the supported YouTube link.
  2. Open DUBSEE, paste the link, and choose the language you want to hear. The App Store build can also be reached from the iOS share sheet; if DUBSEE is missing there, install the App Store version and look again.
  3. Press play and listen on the iPhone. You can switch between the original audio and the dubbing.

If you hear no dubbing

The support notes for this case are specific. Try another video. Check the dubbing language in Settings. On first launch, wait until the advanced voice pack has finished downloading. Deleting the app removes the on-device library, cache, and settings; that is the local reset, not a cloud trash folder.

What the free plan allows while you practice

Free includes 1 video a day, plus up to 2 more after rewarded ads, with about an 18-minute limit per free video. A long lecture may stop at that limit until DubSee Pro removes it. Rewarded ads are part of the free allowance; the privacy policy says an ad plays when you choose to watch one. DubSee Pro also adds 10 voices and background playback with the screen locked. Prices and any trial are shown by the App Store, not guessed on this website.

If you are still deciding whether a video qualifies, read which YouTube videos DUBSEE translates. For what the voice is doing, read AI video dubbing. Contact is on the support page.

Questions

Where do I paste the YouTube link?

In the DUBSEE app on your iPhone, not on this website. You can also look for DUBSEE in the iOS share sheet after the App Store version is installed.

The video opens but there is no dubbing. What should I check?

Try another video, confirm the dubbing language in Settings, and wait if the advanced voice pack is still downloading from the first launch.
