DUBSEE

YouTube video translator for iPhone

DUBSEE translates the speech in supported YouTube videos and plays it with AI dubbing on iPhone. Shorts and live streams are not included.

Bu sayfayı Türkçe oku

DUBSEE is an iPhone app for people who have a YouTube link and want to follow the spoken part in another language. It is not a replacement YouTube player, a news app, or a remote for a television. You bring a link. DUBSEE translates the speech in that video and plays an AI voice while the video runs.

What “supported” means

Support is narrower than “any YouTube URL”. DUBSEE is built for regular YouTube videos that have captions available. Shorts are not supported. Live streams are not supported. A video can still fail when the captions, language, or region do not line up. If one link does not dub, another video is the right next test, not a second tap on the same link.

The App Store description calls the playback real-time dubbing and says the dubbed audio is meant to follow the video. That describes the product’s aim. It is not a promise that every sentence lands perfectly, and it is not an offline mode. DUBSEE needs the video you asked it to open.

Languages you can listen in

The dubbing languages are the same 31 languages published for the app and listed in the menu on this site: English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Português, Nederlands, Polski, Svenska, Dansk, Suomi, Ελληνικά, Čeština, Slovenčina, Slovenščina, Hrvatski, Magyar, Română, Български, Eesti, Latviešu, Lietuvių, Русский, Українська, العربية, 日本語, 한국어, हिन्दी, Bahasa Indonesia, Tiếng Việt. Choosing Turkish on an English lecture, or English on a Turkish lecture, is the same action: paste the link, pick the language you want to hear, and play. The website’s language menu is the list of those dubbing languages. It does not mean every video exists in every language.

Who it is for

The useful cases are lectures, course videos, software tutorials, craft and repair explainers, podcasts posted on YouTube, and other videos where the information is in the speech. Students can stay with the diagram instead of pausing to read. Someone following a tutorial can keep their hands on the work. It is a poor fit if you want clips, live chat, or a general movie library.

Free use and DubSee Pro

The app is free to download. Free use includes 1 video a day, plus up to 2 more after rewarded ads, with about an 18-minute limit per free video. DubSee Pro unlocks unlimited videos, removes that duration limit and the ads, adds 10 voices, and enables background and lock-screen playback. A free trial appears only when the App Store says you are eligible. The price is shown in the app before purchase and can vary by region. DUBSEE is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google.

Questions

Which YouTube videos can DUBSEE translate?

Regular YouTube videos with captions available. Shorts and live streams are not supported. A normal video can still be unavailable because of the captions, language, or region.

Does the translator work for languages other than English?

Yes. DUBSEE dubs supported videos in 31 languages. You choose the language you want to hear after pasting the link.
