How it works

Same flow as in the app — paste or share, then listen.

  1. 1

    Share or paste a link

    From YouTube’s share sheet, or paste a URL on the Link tab.

  2. 2

    Captions become speech

    DubSee reads captions, translates them, and speaks them on your device.

  3. 3

    Watch with dubbing

    Video plays with dubbed audio and captions in your language — offline-friendly, no login.
