How it works

Same flow as in the app — open a link, then listen.

  1. 1

    Share or paste a link

    Use the system share sheet, or paste a URL on the Open tab.

  2. 2

    Dubbing starts on your device

    DubSee prepares natural speech in your dubbing language and plays it with the video.

  3. 3

    Listen while you watch

    Stay in a focused player. Pro keeps playback going in the background and on the lock screen.
