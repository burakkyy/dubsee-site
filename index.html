Translate supported YouTube videos, then listen

Translate supported YouTube video links into your language with DUBSEE. Listen to foreign-language videos with AI-powered dubbing on your iPhone.

DUBSEE is an iPhone app that translates the speech in supported YouTube videos and plays it with AI dubbing in a language you choose. Paste a video link, choose a language, and listen on your iPhone. Regular YouTube videos with captions can work; Shorts and live streams do not. Availability depends on the video, captions, language, and region. Free access is 1 video a day, plus up to 2 more after rewarded ads, with about an 18-minute limit per free video. DubSee Pro removes that duration limit and the ads, and adds unlimited videos, 10 voices, and background playback with the screen locked. DUBSEE is independent and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

How do I translate a YouTube video on iPhone?

Copy a regular YouTube link that has captions, paste it into DUBSEE, choose the language you want to hear, and press play. Shorts and live streams are not supported. Availability depends on the video, captions, language, and region.

Which YouTube videos can I use?

DUBSEE supports regular YouTube videos with available captions. Shorts and live streams are not supported. Availability depends on the video, captions, language, and region.

Is DUBSEE free to try?

DubSee Pro unlocks unlimited videos, removes the duration limit and ads, adds 10 voices, and enables background and lock-screen playback. A free trial appears only when the App Store confirms you are eligible. The price is shown in the app and can vary by region.

Can I listen to foreign-language YouTube videos in English?

Yes, when the video is supported. Choose English in DUBSEE and the spoken content is translated and played with AI dubbing. You can also choose another target language. Regular videos with captions can work; Shorts and live streams cannot.

Does DUBSEE support multiple languages?

Yes. DUBSEE can dub supported videos in 31 languages, including English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, and others. The language menu on this page lists them. These are dubbing languages, not a promise that every video is available in every language.

How does AI video dubbing work?

DUBSEE translates the spoken content of a supported YouTube video into the language you choose and plays an AI voice while the video runs on your iPhone. A voice pack may download on first launch. You can switch between the original audio and the dubbing. It does not work offline, and it does not cover every video.

Is DUBSEE available on iPhone?

Yes. DUBSEE is on the App Store for iPhone, in Photo & Video. Apple’s listing also includes iPad. It is free to download. DubSee Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription, and the price is shown in the app before you buy.

How do I get started with DUBSEE?

Download DUBSEE from the App Store and open it. If a voice pack is still downloading, let it finish. Then copy a supported YouTube link, paste it in, choose a language, and press play. If you hear no dubbing, try another video and check the dubbing language in Settings.
